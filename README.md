
LeadFlow —
Client Lead Management System (Mini CRM)A clean, responsive, and interactive Client Lead Management System (Mini CRM) designed to capture, track, and convert inbound client leads from website contact forms. It features a Unified Dual-Role Portal allowing both Customers and Administrators to interact with the same real-time lead pipeline.Key Features1. Dual-Role Authentication & Demo AccessCustomer Portal Login: Clients can log in to view the real-time progress of their inquiry (New → Contacted → Converted), read updates from their specialist, and send follow-up messages.New Customer Inquiry Registration: Prospective clients without an account can submit an inquiry directly from the login screen to generate a lead in the CRM and immediately access their personal portal.Admin CRM Login: Secure administrative workspace with full CRUD (Create, Read, Update, Delete) pipeline management.One-Click Demo Credential Fill: Built-in credential cards on the login screen allow instant role switching for testing and demonstrations.2. Admin CRM DashboardKPI Summary Metrics: Real-time cards tracking Total Leads, New Leads, Contacted Leads, and Converted Leads (including dynamic conversion rate calculation).Search & Status Filtering: Instant filtering by pipeline status (All, New, Contacted, Converted) and live search by client name, email, or acquisition source.Website Contact Form Simulator: Built-in modal to simulate incoming contact form submissions directly into the CRM pipeline.Inline Status Updates & Slide-Over Drawer: Update lead stages directly from the table or open the slide-over detail drawer to view client metadata, log timestamped follow-up notes, or remove leads.3. Persistent Client-Side StoragePowered by browser localStorage (leadflow_crm_leads_v2 and leadflow_crm_auth_v2), ensuring all status updates, new inquiries, and customer-admin messages stay synchronized across sessions—with a one-click Reset Demo Data option in the Admin header.Demo CredentialsRoleEmailPasswordCustomer Portalsarah@acme.comclient123Admin CRMadmin@leadflow.ioadmin123(Note: Any newly created customer inquiry can also log into the Customer Portal using their submitted email and the demo password client123.)Tech StackFrontend: HTML5, Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)Styling: Tailwind CSS (via CDN) & Google Fonts (Inter)Icons: Lucide IconsState & Persistence: Browser localStorageProject Structure├── index.html      # Complete single-file application (UI, styles, and CRM logic)
